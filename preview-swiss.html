Biography

Engineer and author working at the intersection of agentic AI, network operations, and security — twenty-two years from banking-grade infrastructure to autonomous agents.

The path runs from designing one‑time‑password authentication systems with MD5 hashing in 2004, through enterprise security infrastructure and nationwide e‑channel monitoring of more than 20,000 ATMs, to building and training neural networks with PyTorch and designing autonomous AI agents capable of multi-step reasoning, tool use, and self-correction.

He holds an M.Eng in Network Security from Universitas Indonesia and a B.Eng in Informatics from Universitas Gadjah Mada, has written six books and study guides, and has shipped thirty-three projects — every commit public on GitHub for you to fork & remix.
